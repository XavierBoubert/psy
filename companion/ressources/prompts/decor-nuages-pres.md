LAYER 2 of 4 — NEAR CLOUDS. Bigger, whiter and more defined than the far clouds.

A wide horizontal band of large, plump, brilliant white cumulus clouds across the whole width.

- The clouds occupy the MIDDLE of the frame, roughly the middle third. The top strip and the bottom
  third are FLAT PURE MAGENTA #FF00FF.
- Four or five big cloud masses of clearly different sizes, evenly spread left to right, with wide
  magenta gaps between them. They must not join into one continuous bank.
- No cloud has a straight edge. Every cloud is a complete, closed, rounded puff with lobes all
  the way around it, floating free with magenta on all four sides of it. If a cloud looks like
  it has been sliced by a ruler, vertically or horizontally, the image is wrong.
- Every cloud mass is drawn WHOLE. The leftmost cloud starts a sixth of the way in from the left
  edge, and the rightmost cloud ends a sixth of the way before the right edge: the first sixth and
  the last sixth of the width are empty magenta, top to bottom. Nothing is cut by a side edge.
- Bright soft white, slightly denser than the far layer, with gentle pale blue-white shading inside
  the lobes to give volume. Still no hard edge anywhere: the outline stays soft and slightly fuzzy.
- Big rounded lobes, generous and pillowy.
- A few tiny pale sparkle dots and one or two very small soft light flecks around them. No more.
- Absolutely nothing else: no sky colour, no ground, no foliage, no horizon.
