A single vertical list of chunky panels laid on the painted landscape, top to bottom, full width.
Card text is left-aligned. The horizontal margins are equal on both sides and fairly wide.
The vertical gaps between panels are generous, and the opaque landscape shows through every gap.

THE LANDSCAPE BEHIND THIS SCREEN
- The turquoise sky and its few soft clouds occupy the upper third, the pale-teal meadow the middle,
  and two or three large rounded teal-blue leaves come up from the very bottom edge.
- Those bottom leaves pass BEHIND the panels and are hidden by them — the panels are opaque, nothing
  shows through them. The landscape is visible in the GAPS between panels and in the margins beside them.
- The whole landscape is quiet and evenly lit, and no part of it is more interesting than another.
