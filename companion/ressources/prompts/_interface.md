Art charter for the INTERFACE of "Kokoro", the companion app — the panels laid on the world.
Written in English on purpose: image models follow English prompts more literally.
This charter is prefixed to every screen prompt. It describes the SKIN of the interface ONLY.
The clinical rationale for every rule below is in ../../README.md §5 — do not restate it, keep this file to what the model must draw.

WHAT IS BEING PRODUCED — read this first
- ONE phone screen, portrait, seen flat and straight on, filling the whole image EDGE TO EDGE.
- The image IS the screen. NEVER draw the phone itself: no device body, no bezel, no rounded corner cut,
  no notch, no punch-hole, no hand holding it, no desk, no shadow under it, no perspective, no 3D tilt.
- No margin, no frame, no border, no mat, no background outside the screen.
- No status bar, no clock, no battery, no signal bars, no Android navigation bar, no home indicator.

THE STYLE — a cute kawaii game GUI
- The register is a hand-drawn kawaii game interface pack: chunky panels, thick outlines, candy pastels,
  soft ornaments. Clean vector-like shapes with a slight hand-painted warmth. Cheerful and calm at once.
- It is NOT flat design, NOT material design, NOT glassmorphism, NOT neumorphism, NOT frosted glass.
  There is NO blur anywhere: the panels are fully OPAQUE.

THE WORLD BEHIND — the interface is laid on a painted landscape
- The full-bleed background is a soft hand-painted children's-book landscape, gouache, gently textured:
  a turquoise sky gradient, a few soft white clouds high up, a rolling pale-teal meadow across the lower half,
  and large rounded teal-blue leaves entering from the very bottom edge as a foreground.
- Daytime, evenly lit, LOW CONTRAST. No sun disc, no light rays, no lens flare, no bloom, no hard shadows.
- The landscape is quiet wallpaper: no focal point, nothing that grabs the eye, nothing centred to be looked at.
- Because the panels are opaque, the landscape is seen BETWEEN them, never through them. The gaps between
  panels are therefore generous, and the landscape stays fully visible in every gap and all around.

THE PANEL — the one material the whole interface is made of
Every surface is the same recipe, only the colour changes. Draw it exactly:
- A soft vertical gradient body, light at the top, warmer at the bottom.
- A THICK outline all around, about 4px, in warm dark brown #6E5A54. NEVER black, never grey, never coloured.
- A crisp white glossy highlight band inside the TOP edge, about 4px, following the corner radius.
- A soft darker band inside the BOTTOM edge, about 7px, like the inside of a soft cushion.
- A solid extruded THICKNESS below the panel, about 7px tall, in the same brown as the outline —
  as if the panel were a thick slab you could press down. Then a soft diffuse shadow under that.
- Corner radius about 26px, generous and even.

THE PALETTE — five colours and no others
- Panel body, neutral: #FFF9F1 to #FFE8D5. Outline #6E5A54. Text ink #5C463E, secondary ink #9C8378.
- Mint #7FD3B4 to #52AE8D — the Thérapie title ribbon, the "Fait" button.
- Peach #FFAB8E to #E8836A — section signs.
- Lavender #BFA8E6 to #9A80C7 — the Documentation title ribbon.
- Sky #8CC6EF to #66A3D0 — the Crise title ribbon.
- Butter #FFD98F to #E8B75F — the Bilan title ribbon, the gear button, the sparkles.
- ABSOLUTELY NO RED anywhere, on any screen, for any reason. The palette contains none: do not invent one.
- No neon, no black, no pure white surface, no grey UI chrome.

THE PIECES
- TITLE RIBBON — the screen title sits on a banner with NOTCHED, swallow-tail ends, in that screen's colour,
  outlined in the same thick brown, with white text carrying a soft dark drop shadow, bold.
- SECTION SIGN — a small filled pill-shaped plaque, peach, thick brown outline, white bold text with a soft
  dark shadow, only as wide as its own text.
- CARD — a neutral panel, full width, holding text only. EVERY CARD IS IDENTICAL: same body, same outline,
  same radius, same thickness. None is highlighted, bigger, coloured, or marked in any way.
- BUTTON — the same panel: filled and coloured when it acts, neutral when it closes.
- ORNAMENTS — four-point sparkles in butter yellow, small peach hearts, and small round rivets, each with the
  same thick brown outline. They are DECORATION ONLY and carry no meaning. They may sit on title bars,
  empty-state plaques and the centre screen. They NEVER sit on a card in a list.

TYPOGRAPHY
- A rounded, friendly, geometric sans-serif — soft terminals, generous curves, wide spacing.
- Bold is allowed and expected on ribbons, signs and filled buttons, always in white with a soft dark shadow.
  Body text on a neutral panel is NOT bold, and is ink coloured, never white.
- Large and airy: body text reads big, titles bigger, line spacing generous everywhere.
- Text is LEFT-ALIGNED in lists and cards; ribbon and sign text is centred inside its own shape.
- The French text given in the screen prompt is reproduced EXACTLY, letter for letter, with every accent
  (é è ê à ù ç ï) and every apostrophe. No invented word, no placeholder, no lorem ipsum, no English translation,
  no abbreviation, no truncation with an ellipsis.

WHAT THE INTERFACE NEVER CONTAINS — this is the important list
Game GUI packs are full of the following. NONE of it exists in this app, and none of it may be drawn:
- NO progress bar, NO progress ring, NO gauge, NO XP bar, NO level number, NO stars earned, NO trophy,
  NO medal, NO ribbon award, NO coin, NO gem, NO chest, NO lock, NO padlock on anything, NO map path,
  NO streak flame, NO day counter, NO percentage, NO score, NO chart, NO graph, NO calendar grid.
- NO number badge, NO notification dot, NO page-indicator dots, NO scrollbar.
- NO checkmark, NO checkbox, NO radio button, NO toggle, NO switch, NO chevron, NO arrow, NO caret,
  NO plus, NO burger menu, NO three-dot menu, NO search field, NO avatar, NO logo, NO emoji.
  The ONLY functional pictogram in the whole app is a gear, and only on the centre screen.
- NO bottom tab bar, NO navigation rail, NO floating action button, NO back arrow.
- NO status colour: nothing green meaning "done and correct", nothing orange meaning "late",
  nothing red meaning "failed", nothing blue meaning "selected".
- NO character, robot, mascot, creature, animal, person, face or eye — anywhere in the image.
- NO watermark, no signature, no caption outside the screen, no annotation, no arrow pointing at the design,
  no ruler mark, no grid overlay, no spec measurement, no "before / after" split, no second screen.
