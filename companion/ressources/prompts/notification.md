SUBJECT — one single finished illustration, a WIDE BANNER, used as the background image of an Android
notification. It is not a parallax layer: there is NO magenta anywhere, the picture is complete and opaque
edge to edge. Notification text will be laid on top of its LEFT side by the app.

⚠️ This prompt is used with --sans-base on purpose. It carries its own charter, and it knowingly departs
from prompts/_base.md on three points, at Xavier's explicit request: the tilt, the wink, the V sign.
Everything else below still holds and is not negotiable.

FRAME AND COMPOSITION — the layout is the point, read it twice
- Very wide banner. Everything essential lives in the CENTRAL HORIZONTAL BAND: the top eighth and the
  bottom eighth are plain sky and plain ground and may be cropped away without losing anything.
- LEFT TWO THIRDS: deliberately QUIET AND OPEN. Plain graded sky, one or two soft distant hills low down,
  nothing else. No foliage, no blossom, no cloud detail, no sparkle in that zone — text goes there and
  must stay readable. This emptiness is the subject of the left side, not a mistake.
- RIGHT THIRD: the character, full body, standing on the ground, framed by soft foliage that enters from
  the right edge only. He is small enough to sit inside the band with clear air above his head.
- No border, no frame, no vignette, no rounded corners, no drop shadow at the edges of the image.

THE WORLD — soft hand-painted, this is the decor style of the app
- Soft hand-painted children's-book illustration, gouache / soft digital painting, gently textured.
- Rounded, plump, friendly shapes. Nothing sharp, nothing spiky, nothing angular, nothing threatening.
- LOW CONTRAST, evenly lit, no sun disc, no light rays, no lens flare, no bloom, no harsh highlight,
  no hard-edged shadow. It must read as restful at a glance.
- Palette, and nothing outside it:
  sky and distance turquoise #35B6D8, cyan #55C9E4, pale sky #A9E2EF;
  clouds soft white #FFFFFF fading to pale blue-white #E4F4FA;
  foliage deep teal-blue #1E6E8C, mid teal #2F8BA8, fresh green #6FB98F, pale mint #BFE3D2;
  blossoms, very sparingly, dusty pink #F3A8B8, pale cream #FBEFD8.
- No orange, no red, no purple, no magenta, no brown, no black, no grey, no neon, no metallic.
- Leaves are large and few, never a botanical study. A handful of tiny pale floating dots at most.

THE CHARACTER — Kokoro, a small kawaii robot, drawn in a DIFFERENT style from the world
- Flat 2D vector line art, "ligne claire", laid over the painted world exactly as a sticker would be.
  Uniform stroke, rounded caps, flat fills only. No shading, no gradient, no texture, no glow, no 3D,
  no gloss, no metal sheen, and NO cast shadow on the ground.
- Ink stroke #383838. Shell fill #FAF7F0. Face panel fill #E5DFD4. No other colour in the character:
  he is the only uncoloured thing in the picture, and that is intended.
- Head: a rounded square, slightly WIDER THAN TALL, with an inset rounded rectangular face panel.
  The head is clearly larger than the body. Eyes and mouth exist on that panel and nowhere else.
- Body: a small rounded pear under the head — narrow at the shoulders, wide at the belly — clearly
  smaller than the head but not tiny. One single curved line across the lower body. That line separates
  nothing: it is not a belt, not a hatch, not a dial.
- On the body, ink brush strokes forming the Japanese character 心, placed flat on the body, on the
  character's heart side, which is the viewer's RIGHT. No plate, no badge, no frame around it.
- Feet: two small rounded floating shapes, NO legs drawn between body and feet.
- No neck, no ears, no hair, no clothing, no antenna, no rivet, no gear, no cable, no dial, no chest plate.

THE POSE — the three deliberate departures, draw them exactly as written
- He LEANS towards the viewer's LEFT: the whole figure tilted about 12 degrees, head, body and feet
  tilted together as one, still frontal, no three-quarter turn, no perspective, no foreshortening.
- He WINKS: his left eye — the viewer's RIGHT — stays a solid oval, taller than wide. His other eye is a
  single arc curving UPWARD, a closed smiling eye. The mouth is a small arc curving UPWARD, a calm smile.
  The middle of that arc sits LOWER than its ends. Nothing else on the panel.
- ONE ARM makes a V SIGN: the arm on the viewer's LEFT is raised and ends in a simple mitten-like hand
  with exactly TWO rounded fingers spread into a clear V, palm facing the viewer. Only that one hand
  exists. The other arm stays a plain rounded stump, floating, no hand, no fingers, hanging at rest.

FACE RULES — still absolute, no exception
- NO EYEBROWS. None, ever. No teeth, no tongue, no nose, no cheeks, no blush.
- The eyes never widen, never sparkle, never have highlights, reflections or glints.
- The mouth corners never fall.
- No sweat drop, no tear, no manga emotion symbol, no heart, no star, no sparkle on the character,
  no floating icon, no speech bubble, no motion line.

NEVER DRAW
- Any second character, creature, animal, insect, bird, fish, person or face anywhere in the world.
- Any text, letter, number, caption, watermark, signature, logo, stamp or website address.
  (The 心 on the body is a drawn mark and is the only exception.)
- Any building, path, fence, bench, sign, vehicle, tool or man-made object.
- Any weather event, any night-time or ominous or wilted or thorny or decaying thing.
- Any user interface element, phone mockup, notification mockup, button, icon row or mock screen.
