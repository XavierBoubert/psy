A soft band of distant grass and low foliage running along the BOTTOM of the frame.

- The band occupies only the LOWEST QUARTER of the frame. Everything above it is FLAT PURE MAGENTA #FF00FF.
- Its upper edge is a soft, gently undulating skyline of rounded grass tufts and small leaf clusters —
  slightly taller here, slightly lower there, never a straight line, never a hard cut.
- Mid teal and fresh green, softened and desaturated as distance does, with pale mint at the top edge
  where the light lands. Flat and simple: silhouettes with barely any internal detail.
- A scattering of tiny pale flower dots, dusty pink and pale cream, sitting in the grass. A dozen at most,
  spread evenly, never clustered into a bouquet.
- The band runs unbroken from the left edge to the right edge. No gap, no clearing, no path.
- Absolutely nothing else: no sky, no clouds, no large leaves, no tree, no trunk, no ground plane.
