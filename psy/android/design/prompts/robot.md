SUBJECT — the reference design sheet: the body, the six expressions, the three designation poses.

One single image, three horizontal bands, generous empty margins, nothing else in the frame:
- TOP BAND: the robot alone, full body, front view, centred, at rest, drawn large.
- MIDDLE BAND: a row of six heads, evenly spaced, all the exact same head at the exact same size,
  in the order of the six expressions above. Only the eyes and the mouth change from one head to
  the next. The head itself never changes.
- BOTTOM BAND: three full-body poses of the same robot, one arm extended to designate something
  off to the side — one to the left, one slightly downward to the left, one to the right.
  The extended arm stays at or below the shoulder line in all three.

Landscape image, plain #F4F1EA background, absolutely nothing else in the frame.

The figure must read as a small object that has been set down and is not going anywhere —
settled, quiet, complete. Not waiting for anything, not addressing anyone.
