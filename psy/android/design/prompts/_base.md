Character design charter for "Kokoro", the companion character of a calm mental-health app.
Written in English on purpose: image models follow English prompts more literally.
The clinical rationale for every rule below is in ../CORPS.md — do not restate it here, keep this file to what the model must draw.
This charter is prefixed to every variant prompt. Change it and every future plate changes.

STYLE — non negotiable
- Flat 2D vector line art, "ligne claire". One single uniform stroke weight across the whole drawing, rounded line caps.
- Flat fills only. No shading, no gradient, no drop shadow, no hatching, no texture, no glow, no 3D, no gloss, no metal sheen.
- Strictly front-facing and symmetrical. No perspective, no foreshortening, no tilted axis.
- Background #F4F1EA, completely plain and empty. Stroke #2B2F33. Body fill #FBF9F5. Face panel fill #E6E2DA, a slightly darker warm grey.
- Exactly one accent colour in the whole image: muted celadon #8FA99B, used only for the small chest plate.
  Leave that chest plate EMPTY — a plain celadon rounded square with no symbol, no letter, no glyph inside it.
  Nothing else in the image is coloured.
- Extremely minimal. Fewer lines is always better. No decoration, no props, no background objects, no ground line, no scenery.

THE CHARACTER — a small kawaii robot
- Reads as a small friendly appliance that has been set down, not as a baby and not as a humanoid.
- Head: a rounded square, slightly wider than tall, with an inset rounded rectangular face panel.
  The eyes and the mouth are drawn on that panel and nowhere else.
- Body: a small rounded capsule under the head, clearly smaller than the head but not tiny.
  Plain flat plates only — no rivets, no screws, no gears, no wires, no vents, no seams, no antenna, no dials.
- A small empty celadon rounded plate on the centre of the chest.
- Arms: two short rounded limbs, floating slightly detached from the body, no hands, no fingers.
- Feet: two small rounded shapes, floating slightly detached, no legs drawn between body and feet.
- No neck, no ears, no hair, no clothing, no accessories, no name badge.

BUILT TO BE RIGGED — this constrains the drawing, obey it
- Every part is one separate simple closed shape: head, face panel, left eye, right eye, mouth,
  body, chest plate, left arm, right arm, left foot, right foot.
- Parts touch or float apart, they never interlock, never overlap in a way that would hide one part behind another.
- Each limb is a single shape with no elbow and no knee, so it can be rotated around one pivot without deforming.
- No part is cropped by the frame.

THE SIX EXPRESSIONS — this is the exact list, and nothing outside it
1. Neutral: two solid oval eyes, one short straight horizontal mouth.
2. Attentive: two solid oval eyes, mouth a short flat rounded bar.
3. Warm: eyes are two arcs curving upward, like closed smiling eyes, mouth a small arc curving upward.
4. Blink: eyes are two short straight horizontal lines, mouth a short straight line.
5. Standby: eyes are two arcs curving downward, like closed resting eyes, mouth a short straight line.
6. Looking aside: two solid oval eyes both shifted to the same side of the panel, mouth a short straight line.

FACE RULES — absolute
- NO EYEBROWS. None, on any head, ever.
- The mouth is straight, or a bar, or curved upward. NEVER curved downward, on any head.
- The eyes never widen, never sparkle, never have highlights, reflections or glints.
- No teeth, no tongue, no nose, no cheeks, no blush.
- No sweat drops, no tears, no manga emotion symbols, no hearts, no stars, no sparkles,
  no floating icons, no speech bubbles, no motion lines.

DESIGNATION — the only gesture that exists
- To designate something, one arm extends straight out to the side, with a rounded tip. No hand, no finger, no palm.
- The arm always points away from the viewer's position, at something outside the character. It never points at the viewer.
- The arm never rises above the shoulder line: a raised arm reads as a wave or a raised hand, and that is forbidden.

NEVER DRAW
- Sadness, disappointment, worry, pleading, reproach, or any expression the viewer would have to interpret.
- The robot walking towards the viewer, reaching out to the viewer, waving, thumbs up, applause, any rating gesture.
- Slumped shoulders, lowered head, hunched back, curled-up body.
- Cuteness cues aimed at babies: no oversized head on a tiny body, no big glossy anime eyes.
- Any text, label, caption, number, watermark, signature, logo, border, frame, grid or ruler mark inside the image.
- Any background object, scenery, ground line, cast shadow, phone mockup or user interface element.
