Art charter for the DECOR of "Kokoro", the companion app — the world Kokoro sits in.
Written in English on purpose: image models follow English prompts more literally.
This charter is prefixed to every decor layer prompt. It describes the scenery ONLY.
The character himself is drawn in vector by the app and MUST NOT appear in any of these images.

WHAT IS BEING PRODUCED — read this first
- Each prompt below produces ONE LAYER of a parallax background, not a finished picture.
- The layers are stacked by the app, from far to near, and slide at different speeds.
- So every layer is PARTIAL by design: it covers its own band and leaves the rest EMPTY.

THE EMPTY AREA — non negotiable, this is how the layer gets cut out afterwards
- Everything that is not the subject of the layer is filled with FLAT PURE MAGENTA #FF00FF.
- That magenta is a perfectly uniform, perfectly saturated, perfectly flat solid fill. No gradient
  inside it, no texture, no noise, no vignette, no lighter or darker patch, no glow bleeding into it.
- The magenta is a cut-out mask, not a background to compose against: nothing casts a shadow onto it,
  nothing reflects it, no element is tinted by it, and no element is outlined against it.
- NEVER draw a grey and white checkerboard, and never draw anything that stands for "transparent".
- No sky gradient painted into a layer: the sky is drawn by the app, underneath everything.
- Saturated magenta, fuchsia and violet appear NOWHERE ELSE in the image — not in a leaf, not in a
  shadow, not in a cloud. The blossoms stay PALE and chalky, far away from the mask colour.
- No frame, no border, no vignette, no drop shadow onto the magenta.

STYLE
- Soft hand-painted children's-book illustration, gouache / soft digital painting, gently textured.
- Rounded, plump, friendly shapes. Nothing sharp, nothing spiky, nothing angular, nothing threatening.
- LOW CONTRAST and evenly lit. No harsh highlights, no hard-edged shadows, no sun disc, no light rays,
  no lens flare, no bloom, no glare, no strong specular anything.
- Calm and quiet. The image must read as restful at a glance, with nothing that grabs the eye.
- No single element louder than the rest: no focal point, no subject, no composition centre.

PALETTE — the whole decor lives in this range and nowhere else
- Sky and distance: turquoise #35B6D8, cyan #55C9E4, pale sky #A9E2EF.
- Clouds: soft white #FFFFFF fading to very pale blue-white #E4F4FA.
- Foliage: deep teal-blue #1E6E8C, mid teal #2F8BA8, fresh green accents #6FB98F, pale mint #BFE3D2.
- Blossoms, sparingly: dusty pink #F3A8B8, pale cream #FBEFD8.
- No orange, no red, no purple, no brown, no black, no grey, no neon, no metallic.

DENSITY — this is a background, it must stay quiet
- Simple silhouettes with a few internal details, never a botanical study.
- Leaves are large and few, not thousands of small ones.
- Sparkles and floating dots: a handful at most, tiny, pale, evenly scattered. Never a swarm.

COMPOSITION — the layers get mirrored and tiled sideways by the app
- The artwork runs edge to edge horizontally and is evenly spread across the full width.
- No object is centred, no object is a hero, nothing is placed to be looked at.
- Nothing important touches the left or right edge in a way that would be missed if cut.
- Flat and frontal. No perspective lines, no vanishing point, no horizon line drawn as a hard edge.

NEVER DRAW
- Any character, creature, animal, insect, bird, fish, person, face, eye, robot or mascot. None. Ever.
- Any text, letter, number, caption, watermark, signature, logo, stamp or website address.
- Any building, path, fence, bench, sign, vehicle, tool or man-made object.
- Any weather event: rain, storm, lightning, snow, wind lines, motion streaks.
- Anything dark, night-time, ominous, wilted, dead, thorny or decaying.
- Any user interface element, phone mockup, frame, mock screen, border or margin decoration.
